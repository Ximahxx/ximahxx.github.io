Chapter 5 Core Summary Notes

High-yield study points from ICAN Business Environment Study Text.

5.1 Business Uses of Computers & IT Systems

  • Database Systems: Structured collections of data managed by DBMS for efficient storage, retrieval, and integrity.
  • Accounting Software: Automates financial record keeping, ledgers, accounts payable/receivable, and statutory reporting.
  • Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP): Integrated software systems (e.g., SAP, Oracle) that streamline and coordinate all business operations across departments.
  • CRM (Customer Relationship Management): Systems designed to manage interactions with current and potential customers to boost retention and sales.
  • Cybersecurity Risks: Malware, ransomware, phishing, and data breaches. Mitigations: Strong passwords, multi-factor authentication (MFA), regular backups, employee training, and robust IT security policies.

5.2 E-Commerce & E-Business

  • E-Commerce Models: B2B (Business-to-Business), B2C (Business-to-Consumer), C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer), and C2B.
  • Digitisation of the Middleman: Platforms like Amazon, eBay, Konga, and Jiji connect buyers and sellers directly, reducing friction and redefining retail distribution.
  • Data Centre Disaster Recovery: Contingency planning, offsite backups, and redundant systems to ensure business continuity during cyber attacks or power outages.

5.3 Emerging Technologies & Accounting

  • Cloud Computing: On-demand delivery of computing services over the internet (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), offering scalability and remote access.
  • Blockchain: A decentralized, immutable digital ledger that records transactions across a network, ensuring transparency and security in banking and supply chains.
  • Fintech: Technology-enabled financial innovations (mobile money, peer-to-peer lending, robo-advisors) transforming traditional banking.
  • Internet of Things (IoT): Network of physical objects embedded with sensors and software to exchange data and track supply chain assets in real time.
